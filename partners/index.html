Partner Program FAQ

Do I need technical knowledge to become a partner?

No. Basic product understanding is enough. Our team can support you with demos, onboarding, and technical guidance.

How do I earn commission?

You earn commission when an eligible customer referred or onboarded by you successfully subscribes to Connektly.

Who can I refer?

You can refer businesses that use WhatsApp, Instagram, or Messenger for sales, support, marketing, or customer communication.

Will Connektly help me close customers?

Yes. Our team can assist with product demos, onboarding support, and platform-related questions.

Is this suitable for agencies?

Yes. Agencies can offer Connektly as an additional solution to their clients and create a new revenue stream.
