Frequently Asked Questions

Is Connektly an official WhatsApp CRM?

Connektly connects with WhatsApp Cloud API and helps businesses manage WhatsApp conversations, leads, campaigns, and automations from one dashboard.

Can I send WhatsApp campaigns?

Yes. You can send WhatsApp campaigns using approved template messages to selected contacts or CSV recipient lists.

Can my team use Connektly together?

Yes. You can invite team members, assign roles, and allocate leads or contacts to specific team members.

Can I automate WhatsApp replies?

Yes. Connektly supports rule-based WhatsApp automations such as keyword triggers, lead-created triggers, and automated message actions.

Does Connektly support Instagram and Messenger?

Yes, higher plans support a unified inbox for WhatsApp, Instagram, and Messenger conversations.

Do I need technical knowledge to use Connektly?

No. Connektly is designed for business teams. Setup can be guided, and most core workflows are no-code.
