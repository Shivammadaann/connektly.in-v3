WhatsApp API

The **WhatsApp Business App** is a basic, free mobile application designed strictly for small, local merchants with low chat volumes. It is limited to one primary phone and up to four linked devices, lacking automation pipelines, deep CRM sync, template schedulers, or scalable bulk campaigns.

The **WhatsApp Business API** is built for growing and enterprise scale teams. It provides advanced developers and operations managers with the capability to integrate WhatsApp directly into custom software, support desks, and CRM databases. It enables multi-agent collaboration, smart AI chatbots, automated high-throughput routing, and mass broadcasts. Connektly acts as the premium interface and backend server layer for Meta's official API.

Pricing & Billing

Connektly plans are billed on a flexible monthly or heavily discounted annual cycle. The platform fee covers core platform server operations, dashboard metrics, unlimited agent logins, automation scripts, CRM integration hooks, and visual templates management features.

There are absolutely no hidden platform transaction charges or markup rates on our software. The exact subscription price is determined by the specific workflow plan (Developer, Business, or Enterprise) that your team chooses.

Meta charges for official WhatsApp Business API usage on a per-conversation basis rather than a per-message basis. A single conversation is a 24-hour continuous window that begins the moment you reply to a customer query or send a business-initiated template alert.

These conversations are billed under four distinct categories: Utility, Authentication, Marketing, and Service. Each category has its own specific regional pricing set directly by Meta. Connektly routes these usage fees directly to your Meta billing setup with **zero additional platform markup**, ensuring complete financial transparency.

Yes. You have total freedom to upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your Connektly platform subscription plan at any time from your account billing tab.
