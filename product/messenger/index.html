Frequently Asked Questions

What do I need to connect Facebook Messenger to Connektly?

You simply need an active Facebook Page. Once linked via our seamless integration, Connektly will automatically sync your messages and allow you to manage interactions from our unified inbox.

Are there any messaging limits on Facebook Messenger?

Facebook enforces a standard 24-hour messaging window. After a user sends a message to your Page, you have 24 hours to reply with any content. Outside this window, you can use Message Tags or One-Time Notifications (OTN) for specific follow-ups.

Can I build AI agents for Facebook Messenger?
