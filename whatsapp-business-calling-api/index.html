What is the WhatsApp Business Calling API?

The WhatsApp Business Calling API is Meta's official voice extension to the WhatsApp Business Platform. It allows businesses to make and receive voice calls inside the same WhatsApp thread customers already use to message, with no separate phone line, no app switching, and no carrier charges on incoming calls.

Connektly is one of the official WhatsApp Tech Partners authorized by Meta to bring the WhatsApp Business Calling API to your team. Once your verified WhatsApp number is connected, your customers can tap the call icon in WhatsApp to reach you, and you can call them back with consent, using your verified business name and badge on display.

WhatsApp Business Calling API: Pricing & Permissions

Incoming calls

Free for both your business and the customer, in every supported country.

Outgoing calls

Billed per minute by Meta in 6-second increments. The rate depends on the destination country and your monthly volume tier.

Permission rules

One outbound call request per customer per 24 hours, max two in 7 days. Once approved, the call must happen within 72 hours.
